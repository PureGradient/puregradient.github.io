The latest happenings in the world of PureGradient

Samantha Lawson · Sep 9, 2024

We kicked off the autumn with a bang by attending two awesome conferences. The AIIH conference in Swansea, Wales blew our minds with its groundbreaking insights, particularly on leveraging multi-condition model algorithms for handling chronic multi-condition cases. And let's not forget the Research Software Engineering conference, where we dived into the nitty-gritty of the community's future and geeked out in some seriously technical workshops and hackathons.

But that's not all – we also had the privilege of chatting with a group of talented CTO program students. Our discussion delved deep into the business and career journey of becoming a CTO, emphasizing the critical non-technical aspects.

Exciting times are ahead as we join forces with a consortium of innovative partners to create a cutting-edge, medically validated platform for digital health management and application marketplace.

We didn't stop there. We took a deep dive into Microsoft's data science and analytics platforms, explored Synapse and Fabric, and discovered that Microsoft's strategy revolves around unifying at an infrastructure level. This promises enhanced performance and true integration of all data types, along with a simplified approach to data storage and retrieval.

Highlighting the importance of information and infrastructure security, we completed our ISC2 training and are poised for certification. Ensuring the strictest security policies and protocols is paramount to us, especially in our health and medicine-related research and support endeavors.

Last but not least, we're laser-focused on implementing learning algorithms in our internal research to optimize disease classification methodologies. The future is looking bright at PureGradient!
